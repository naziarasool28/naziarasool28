How Delivery Actually Runs

The tools I set up on day one

Part of fractional PM work is arriving at a startup with no process and standing one up fast. Here's the stack I configure and run for client teams — sprint tracking, stakeholder communication, cloud infrastructure, and the no-code platforms behind fast MVP builds.

Delivery & Collaboration

Sprint tracking & stakeholder communication

Standardized workflows and automated reporting — the setup that cut planning overhead by 5+ hours a week on my current engagement.

Jira

Sprint planning, backlog grooming, and release tracking — usually split into separate developer and operations workspaces for clarity.

Trello

Lightweight boards for smaller teams and early-stage founders who don't need Jira's overhead yet.

Microsoft Teams

Daily standups, stakeholder updates, and async delivery reporting for founders and leadership.

Claude (AI)

AI-assisted documentation and delivery support inside the Scrum framework — part of how I keep planning overhead down.

Cloud Infrastructure

Multi-cloud coordination

I work directly with DevOps across all three major providers — currently maintaining 99.9% uptime while cutting monthly cloud costs by 15% through resource optimization.

Microsoft Azure

Core infrastructure coordination and cost tracking alongside engineering and DevOps.

AWS

Resource optimization and uptime monitoring for production workloads.

Google Cloud

Additional infrastructure alignment for teams running multi-cloud by design.

No-Code & Low-Code

Fast, disciplined MVP builds

For founders who need to launch in weeks, not quarters — the same scoping, QA, and UAT rigor as a traditional engineering build.

Bubble

Full-featured web app builds for MVPs with real backend logic.

FlutterFlow

Cross-platform mobile MVPs, scoped and shipped on a fixed timeline.

Webflow

Marketing sites and front-ends that need to look production-ready fast.

Retool

Internal tools and admin panels — often the fastest way to unblock an operations team.
